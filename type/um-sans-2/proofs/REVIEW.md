# Alpha 0 review record

## Required sizes

- 16 px: reject if counters close or `rn` becomes `m`;
- 24 px: interface and compact heading proof;
- 42 px: mobile hero proof;
- 58 px: desktop hero proof;
- 96 px: contour and spacing inspection;
- A4 print at 12, 18, 36 and 72 pt.

## Required environments

- Chromium macOS and Android viewport;
- Safari macOS and iOS viewport;
- Firefox desktop;
- PDF export and office document embedding;
- grayscale and low-contrast display.

## Promotion checklist

- [ ] Every control string reviewed at all required sizes.
- [ ] No collision, malformed join or accidental counter.
- [ ] Spanish accents share height and optical alignment.
- [ ] Figures align in both proportional and tabular modes.
- [ ] No CSS tracking or transform used to disguise the drawing.
- [ ] Two independent reviewers sign the proof.
