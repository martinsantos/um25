# UM Sans 2.0 Original: design and delivery brief

## Objective

Create an independently drawn Latin editorial family for ULTIMA MILLA with the
technical clarity of 1.x, a more ownable construction and documented legal
provenance. This is a separate type-design project, not an automated transform
of Inter.

## Required source deliverables

- native Glyphs or UFO masters plus `designspace` and feature sources;
- at least Text Roman, Display Roman, Text Italic and Display Italic masters;
- independent outlines for alphabet, figures, punctuation and symbols;
- anchors, composites, mark positioning, kerning classes and interpolation;
- Git history showing drawing decisions and contributor authorship;
- signed contributor/license records and similarity/trademark review.

## Design requirements

- distinctive but readable `a`, `g`, `G`, `R`, `M`, `&`, `I/l/1` and `O/0`;
- rounded, assertive 700–900 display color without closed counters;
- genuine italic construction, not slanted Roman;
- Spanish-first diacritics and punctuation designed at every master;
- body rhythm that works at 16–20 px without negative tracking;
- tabular technical figures and stable monetary/date alignment;
- optical-size behavior designed in the masters, not simulated after export.

## Market release

The 2.0 package must contain everything in the 1.2 release plus source masters,
designspace, feature sources, proof history, legal chain of title, migration
notes, beta feedback, application/device tests and a new licensing decision.

## Exit criteria

2.0 may be called an original market typeface only when an independent review
can reproduce the family from those masters and confirm that no upstream
outlines were copied. Until then, 1.2 remains the definitive corporate OFL
release and the word “original” describes UMSA modifications, not the skeleton.
