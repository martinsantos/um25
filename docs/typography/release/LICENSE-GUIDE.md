# License guide

UM Sans is licensed under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL)**. The complete
license is `OFL-1.1.txt` and is the controlling legal document.

## Permitted

- use in commercial and non-commercial documents, apps, websites and video;
- embed in PDFs and software subject to the OFL;
- redistribute the font with the OFL notice;
- modify and redistribute it under the OFL using a non-conflicting family name.

## Required when redistributing

- keep the OFL license and copyright notices with the fonts;
- keep Modified Versions under OFL 1.1;
- do not imply endorsement by The Inter Project Authors or ULTIMA MILLA;
- respect the `UM Sans` and ULTIMA MILLA trademarks.

## Commercial sale and EULA

The OFL permits charging for integration, support, customization, media or a
larger software/package that includes the fonts. The font software may not be
sold by itself as the sole product. A proprietary EULA must not contradict or
replace the OFL for these binaries. `EULA-NOTICE.md` records that boundary for
procurement and legal review.

This guide is operational documentation, not legal advice.
