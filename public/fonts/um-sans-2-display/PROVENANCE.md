# UM Sans 2 Display: blocked drawing experiment

These procedural outlines were generated locally without opening, subsetting
or transforming a third-party font. Structural validity does not establish
typographic quality: word-level raster review failed because of malformed
contours, inconsistent accents and unacceptable spacing rhythm.

Approved use: none. The binaries remain only as diagnostic evidence and must
not be loaded, embedded, distributed or presented as an ULTIMA MILLA typeface.
A future original display family requires a complete professional redraw plus
independent authorship, similarity, trademark, platform and visual reviews.
