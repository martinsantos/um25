## 🎨 POST-PROCESAMIENTO RECOMENDADO

Si las imágenes generadas aún tienen bordes visibles:

1. **En Photoshop/GIMP:**
   - Select > Color Range > seleccionar fondo
   - Feather 2-3px
   - Delete
   - Export as PNG with transparency

2. **En Canva:**
   - Background Remover tool
   - Export PNG transparent

3. **Con remove.bg:**
   - Upload image
   - Download PNG transparent
